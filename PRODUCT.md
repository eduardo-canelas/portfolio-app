# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, technical interviewers, and early-stage product teams reviewing Eduardo Canelas Eterovic for junior software engineering roles. They are scanning quickly, often from LinkedIn or GitHub, and need to understand project quality, technical range, and ownership without reading every repository.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Eduardo as a product-minded junior software engineer with credible full-stack, AI, database, logistics, Java, and systems coursework. Success means a recruiter can identify the strongest projects, open the best GitHub links, download the CV, and contact Eduardo within one focused session.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, builder-focused. The tone should feel confident and technical without sounding corporate or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid template portfolio grids, oversized centered hero copy, generic neon developer aesthetics, fake metrics, emoji decoration, and equal-card project rows that make every repository look equally important.

## Design Principles

- Lead with proof: the best projects should surface before generic biography.
- Make scanning easy: every section should answer what was built, what stack was used, and why it matters.
- Use motion as evidence of craft: animations should support orientation and feedback, not distract from recruiting goals.
- Show judgment: separate standout production-style projects from smaller practice repositories.
- Keep identity present: use Eduardo's real photo instead of placeholder initials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-readable navigation, meaningful alt text, responsive single-column mobile layouts, and reduced-motion support for GSAP animations.
